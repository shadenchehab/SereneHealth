Problem Statement

Serene Health is evaluating DevRev as the AI platform across two brands: TrueCare (patient RPM app) and Alliance (HIE for hospitals, clinics, health plans, IPAs, and ACOs). Support today sits in Zoho. The client asked whether DevRev can replace Zoho feature by feature, and wants to trust-but-verify in a live workspace. Prior demos landed: similar-ticket search — “bring me all the issues that are related” — returning related work as knowledge. Next they want organization knowledge from Microsoft 365 email, OneDrive, and SharePoint, and to see Agent Studio as the way to build agents quickly (the Power Automate comparison), so this is an AI platform going forward, not a single bot.

Ticket volumes, KB size, exact Zoho gap list, Alliance-specific workflows, and success metrics were not locked in this meeting. Pending
