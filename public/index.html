DevRev × Serene Health — TrueCare App and Alliance. Structured PoC for Computer (enterprise search and team assist), CX Agent on the TrueCare Help screen and Support team access for Alliance and TrueCare.

Scope

Problem Statement & Scope of Work

TrueCare patient app and Alliance provider platform three PoC use cases.

Problem Statement

Support knowledge is siloed across tools, so teams struggle with complex report generation and have low visibility into related tickets and past resolutions. Serene Health needs one AI platform for smarter TrueCare and Alliance internal support, and a CX chat AI agent that can help patients with their questions.

In Scope

Three Use Cases

Use Case 1

CX Agent — TrueCare patients

PLuG on the TrueCare Help screen (Chat). With visibilty to historical conversation and ticket status.

Use Case 2

Computer & Agent Studio

Similar-ticket search and org knowledge for executives and support. Agent Studio as the AI platform to build automation and agents that share trusted answer everytime

Use Case 3

Zoho replacement — Support

Support app for TrueCare and Alliance teams replace SaaS with AI based support solution for better intake, routing, and resolution.
